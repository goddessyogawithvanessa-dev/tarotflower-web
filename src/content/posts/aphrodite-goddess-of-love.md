Aphrodite, the ancient Greek Goddess of **love and beauty**, embodies the essence of the flowering **divine feminine**. In one of the best-known Greek traditions, she was born from sea foam and came ashore in Cyprus. In Tarot Flower's poetic visualization of her, she is surrounded by seashells, mermaid companions, and flowers. She gazes into a hand-held mirror, brushing her long and luxurious hair next to the waters of the sea.

The Goddess Aphrodite represents the love and beauty within every one of us. In Tarot Flower's modern embodiment practice, she is associated with the **heart chakra**. Learn how to embody this Goddess so that you too can radiate beauty and love. Working with her symbolism can invite **abundance and joy** as your heart chakra begins to blossom.

Gently walk with your heart and **authenticity**, your truth, and really own it and **be your own beloved**. Be your own Goddess of love. You don’t need to seek it from the outside world.

## Aphrodite the Goddess of Love

As the Greek Goddess of love, Aphrodite will teach you how to listen to the whispers of your heart. As you reconnect with your heart chakra and your own **self-love and love for others**, the Goddess Aphrodite will become embodied in you.

For a mythology-focused profile—including Aphrodite's sea-born origins, her relationship to Venus, and her Oracle meaning—read [The Goddess Aphrodite: Embodying Beauty of All Kinds](/goddess-aphrodite/).

The voice of the heart has always been there with you, it’s always been your honest, deepest soul self. It is the same voice that you had with you when you were a little child. It hasn’t changed. Your soul hasn’t changed.

But as we grow up and we learn things - and we are conditioned - we start to question. We start to think. So, return to the voice of the heart, find the voice of your inner child, and learn to distinguish it from the other voices.

## Why We Should Listen to the Voice of the Heart

So, why is it important to listen to the voice of the heart? It can be the **first step to aligning to your identity**, to **manifestation**, to finding your **desires** and your **truths**. That is why it is so critical to come into that heart space and connect to that loving place, so that we become the observers of our thoughts, our past, our conditioning, and our programming. It is a fundamental part of [Goddess embodiment—connecting to your inner divine feminine](/goddess-embodiment-practices/).

## Goddess of the Heart Chakra

Working with the inspiration of the Goddess Aphrodite can support a **heart-centered practice**. As you embody this goddess of love, you may begin to find your inner light and your true identity. You will not only radiate love, but you will also shine with self-love. By embodying love, we can create more space for loving connections in our lives.

Aphrodite can also help you to learn how to anchor yourself in love before you journey into **shadow work**. It is important to remain connected to our heart chakra as we explore any energies that might be in shadow resonance, so that we can meet vulnerable material without collapsing into self-judgment or shame.

Returning to the beauty and love of the radiant heart can support **healing from within**. This way, you can keep your inner light shining, even in the darkest places.

## Aphrodite Embodiment Dance

Dance your way into love and alignment and move like the Goddess Aphrodite. Through embodiment dance, you can explore a heart-warming, joyful, heart-centered connection with her.

Approach the dance with this intention. Each move can carry a purpose while cultivating the divine feminine.

As you dance, embody the flowing and loving qualities of the feminine. Bring in the heart and integrate it through water movements.

The **water element** is a beautiful symbol of flow and renewal within the body. When you move with or imagine water, you can visualize energy moving through you. Let the practice invite stagnant, stuck energy to release through the body. Our heart is like a flower, gently opening its petals.

## Aphrodite Goddess Story

Aphrodite, Goddess of love, can inspire you toward a heart-centered opening and the embodiment of beauty and divine feminine essence. The following **goddess story** is a Tarot Flower poetic visualization inspired by Aphrodite, rather than a retelling of a documented ancient Greek myth.

### Aphrodite Goddess of Love

*"Once upon a time there was a beautiful goddess named Aphrodite who was birthed right out of the waters of the Mediterranean sea. There she was, resting on the shells and rocks by the ocean with her mermaid sisters and friends. Embracing all of her naturalness, rawness and freedom.*

*Aphrodite sat there every day basking and luxuriating in all of her essence and love and beauty. She was alluring and magnetizing in every way. Her mermaid sisters were completely inspired by her essence. They would braid her hair and adorn her with pink and red roses celebrating her divinity. As Aphrodite held her mirror out, she was mesmerized and totally in awe of her own reflection, her innate divine beauty inside and out.*

*Goddess Aphrodite is there to remind you to embody this divine love and beauty also. Listen to her whispers as she is here to inspire you to know that you too are your own Goddess of beauty and divine love."*

## Nourishment for the Goddess of Love

Nourishing, healthy foods can become part of a heart-centered embodiment practice. This holistic approach is an important aspect of embodied learning. We can go beyond trying to address everything with the mind and recognize that healing and happiness come in many forms.

We can support ourselves with **guided visual meditations**, **embodiment dance**, **journaling**, and **heart chakra affirmations**. We can also nourish body, mind, and soul through the foods we put into our bodies. Consider which nourishing foods feel aligned with the Goddess Aphrodite and help you feel like your own healthy and radiant goddess of beauty.

When we practice self-love, it is important to express gratitude for all that we have learned and accomplished. Taking time to prepare and enjoy nourishing food can become one way of honoring ourselves and all the ways in which we embody the Goddess within us.

## Aphrodite Heart Chakra Affirmations

What would an Aphrodite embodiment practice be without the use of heart chakra affirmations? Within this modern practice, Aphrodite's archetype invites us toward self-love.

When you do affirmations you are consciously taking the time to choose words to alter the way you perceive yourself. However, choosing words that **uplift** and **empower** your divineness, does not come in isolation. Just because you say to yourself “I am love” or “I am enough,” for example,  does not necessarily mean it is true or that you really believe this.

## Heart Chakra Affirmations That Work

Affirmations begin as consciously chosen words, but repeating them does not automatically make them feel true. They become more meaningful when you connect them with genuine feeling. Embodiment practices can reinforce that dialogue within the body and cultivate what might be described poetically as a deep cellular knowing that you are enough or that you are love. Your “deep knowing” lies in the feeling within the body.

If we want to radiate self-love and beauty the way that the Goddess Aphrodite does, then we need to learn how to embody these heart chakra affirmations.
