It happens to everyone - we set intentions, manifest, do everything right… and nothing happens. What if there was a way to amplify your magic? What if you could magnify your intention?

There’s something that a lot of witches miss. If we only manifest with our minds and psychic powers, we abdicate the power that our body brings. Intentions and psychic powers are all in the headspace.

What if you fueled your intentions with the other chakras? The sacral, the solar plexus, the root?

What happens when we fuel our intention from within and below?

## How Does Magic Actually Work?

Magic begins when we realize that life doesn’t happen to us, but *we happen to life*. We all carry inherent energy, and when we learn to focus, curate and target that energy toward a desired outcome, we are making magic. It sounds simpler than it is - magic can be chaotic. Unprocessed emotions and [unintegrated shadows](https://tarotflower.com/goddess-embodiment-practices/) will fragment our work. But, once we work from a place of pure intention, the natural energy available to all of us can be wielded toward a clear purpose. This is magic. And the studied practice of this is - [ritual](https://tarotflower.com/rituals/).

## How Do Rituals and Spells Actually Work?

A ritual is like a guide - a format that will help you wield your magic. Spells are like a recipe - the tools and ingredients you can use to bring an intention into being. Spells can give you the ingredients, the incantations, the direction, but if you don’t know how to cook - if you don’t know how to turn on the stove - the recipe won’t do you much good. The ritual formalizes the choreography, the spell gives you the recipe, but you need to know how to light the flame in the first place.

## Why Aren't My Spells or Manifestations Working?

We’ve all felt that frustration when we try to will something into being, but the universe doesn’t respond. And usually it’s because there are fragments of energies that are polluting our intention. Powerful emotions can create a lot of magical energy, but if you are not pure in your intention, the result may be something nobody could have expected.

If you are longing for love, for someone, [a twin flame perhaps](https://tarotflower.com/how-to-survive-the-twin-flame-separation/) - what energetic baseline are you activating from? Longing? Lack? Loneliness? Grief? Those energies will multiply when you engage your fire.

You must manifest and create from a neutral space. That doesn’t mean you can’t have feelings! It means that when you [wield the fire](https://tarotflower.com/step-into-your-fire/), you must first leave your shadows behind. What this shadow work means is integrating your shadows so that they no longer have an emotional hold over you. This is deep work and it isn’t easy, but it allows you to practice your magic from a place of neutrality.

When we initiate our manifestation from a place of purity, stasis and love, we can more safely grow and wield the power that we are raising. In this way, we reduce the risk of unanticipated outcomes or unintended effects.

## How Can I Make My Magic More Powerful?

There is a beautiful way that you can magnify the power of your magic - by embodying it! Why do you think the history books talk about witches dancing around the fire? Not because it is evil, my goddess, but because it is powerful! Society has taught us to sever our minds from our bodies for generations. But that is only to contain and restrain us.

The energy we create when we move with intention is boundless! And that is the secret you are missing.

In order to raise the energy for a spell, you must first visualize your intention as a light being, a thought, a dream outside of yourself - in front of yourself. See it into being and then draw it into your body. Fuel it with your inner fire, pull it through your bones, and when you begin to move - feel it grow inside of you.

Every movement gives it power! As you embody the feeling, the essence of your intention, you can stoke that fire with your dance. Feel it, nurture it, light it up! Grow it, fuel it, and pull that energy through your body and raise it up to the skies!

That is raising the energy, my Goddess!

## How Can I Strengthen My Magical Intentions?

The key to strengthening your intention is purity and simplicity. Try to write it in one word. Try to refine it down to its essence. The clearer your intention, the more direct it will be. But if you really want to supercharge that power - dance!

Any time you [activate your intention through embodiment practice](https://tarotflower.com/5-tips-affirmations-to-manifest-abundance/) you will magnify its power! It’s difficult to explain - it’s something you have to *feel*. And it is exactly in that feeling that your power grows. Imagine your intention radiating out of your body. It’s no longer just a thought, but a force you can send into the world. Think about the power of ecstasy - now imagine your intention riding on that wave of energy.

You will feel it! You will know. And when you send it out to the universe it will grow threefold. Three times three, sister.

## Why Do We Use Embodiment Dance in Ritual and Magic?

Our body is our most blessed vessel. We can create heat, we can move through space - all of this energy inside and around us affects our intention. What was once just a thought, a dream, an idea becomes physical - real in your space. And you can raise, raise, raise, your temple of energy!

[Embodiment dance](https://tarotflower.com/embodiment-dance-for-high-vibe-living/) connects us to all of our senses - touch, taste, smell, sound, sight. We engage [all of the elements - earth, air, fire, water](https://tarotflower.com/creating-sacred-space-how-to-cast-a-goddess-circle/), into a beautiful dance of aether. The elements are us, and we are of the elements, and we can use their power to create our magic. But first, we must embody them.

*“An ye harm none, do what ye will”*

This is an often contested and misunderstood principle, but in the Wiccan Rede, the rule is simple: As long as you are not harming anyone else, you may practice as you like. But simple doesn’t always mean easy. Ask yourselves these questions:

*If you are influencing someone else’s free will, could you be harming them?*

*If you are using magic to change someone else’s behavior, aren’t you robbing them of their own choices?*

This is why the safest and best practice is to use your magic upon and for yourself. If you want to attract love, cast a spell to bring love into your own life. Make yourself more desirable, grow the energy of love in your own energetic field. Then you will not run the risk of unwittingly harming another.

## Can Magic Help Me Reunite With My Twin Flame?

Yes, but not in the way you will expect. This is the hard part, Goddess. It’s the part nobody wants to hear. Your twin flame came into your life to hold up a mirror to your soul - and you to them. Try to think of your twin flame as an energy instead of a person. Try to detach the 3D aspects of them from the message.

What is it that you love so much about them?

What is it that lights you up so much when you are in their arms?

What is it that they make you *feel* when you are together?

Name it. Write it down. This is what the universe is asking you to give to yourself. Your twin flame is your [mirror soul](https://tarotflower.com/what-is-a-twin-flame-how-to-know-if-youve-met-your-mirror-soul/). You are two parts of the whole. And when you find your other half within yourself, you will be reunited. Perhaps not in the physical world - but trust me Goddess - once you achieve this, it won’t torment you anymore. You will be whole in love within yourself. I know it’s almost impossible to imagine. The pain is so deep. But you are becoming. Stay strong.

## Why Isn't My Twin Flame Manifestation Working?

The hardest part of the twin flame journey is realizing that it was never about them. It was always about you.

I know it’s brutal, but until you integrate the shadows and give to yourself what you see in your twin flame, you cannot come together. Like two magnets turned the wrong way, you will repel each other until each of you become whole.

Sometimes one twin won’t do the work. Sometimes they will run forever. But you have the chance to heal and become the most beautiful version of yourself.

And dare I say - you may even forget them. Not forever. Never forever. But it may become a beautiful memory of the most gorgeous painful gift you ever received.

The chance to return to yourself.

## How Can I Stop Obsessing Over My Twin Flame?

Twin flame obsession is incredibly difficult to avoid. Often you can feel their presence constantly around you. They invade your dreams. They suddenly pop into your awareness even if you don’t want them to.

When this happens, stop and reflect on what you are currently doing. What is happening around you? What were you feeling or doing in that moment? Try to look for the message in this. Rather than attaching this feeling to the 3D person that mirrors you, communicate with the divine masculine or divine feminine energy directly. What is it teaching you in this moment?

[Focus on your own becoming, Goddess.](https://tarotflower.com/sacred-becoming/) Build your fire, your flame. Love yourself with as much passion as you do your twin flame and see where it takes you. We cannot find true love from a place of longing and pain.
