## Body Mind & Soul Practices for the Divine Feminine

![Vanessa Hylande meditating with closed eyes, a stillness practice in Tarot Flower’s Goddess Embodiment guide](/images/2023/10/Meditation-1.jpg)

Hi! I'm Vanessa Hylande, the soul behind Tarot Flower and the Goddess Yoga Method. Goddess Embodiment is my methodology for bringing archetype, body, mind, spirit and creative practice into lived experience—a personal journey to unlock your inner power and confidence.

The methodology works through:

**1. Strengthening the physical body through yoga, dance, and body conditioning.**  
**2. Exploring the mind through [sound bath meditations](/sound-bath-meditations/), shadow work, journaling, and reflection.**  
**3. Connecting to spirit through intuitive tarot card readings, Goddess channeling, moon magic rituals, and more.**  
**4. Bringing the practice into shared experience, where desired. [Goddess Yoga Retreats gatherings](https://goddess-yoga-retreats.com/our-gatherings/) are one place where this work can come alive in community.**

I'm a **certified yoga teacher**, a former ballet dancer at the **Metropolitan Opera in NYC**, and a professional opera singer. But above all, I'm a woman and a mother who has journeyed through betrayal and grief to find joy, friendship, community, and unconditional love.

Welcome, Sister! It's time to embrace your divine potential.

## The Goddess Embodiment Practice

![Vanessa Hylande stretching with raised arms in Tarot Flower’s Goddess Embodiment movement practice](/images/2023/09/1-1.jpg)

Goddess yoga, dance and **body conditioning** bring the methodology into movement, adapted to your experience level and time availability. The practice invites you to move with grace, attend to your body's limits, and develop an understanding of the goddess yoga and dance method. For everyday ways to begin, explore [Feminine Embodiment Practices to Shine](/feminine-embodiment-practices-to-shine/).

To learn more about how we can explore the chakras through movement, check out [Embodiment Dance for High Vibe Living](/embodiment-dance-for-high-vibe-living/), where we talk about embodiment dance and its relation to the seven chakras.

**Shadow work** and **inner child healing** bring sound bath meditations, journaling exercises and self-empowerment strategies into reflection. Whether it is a negative inner dialogue or unconscious beliefs, these practices offer ways to explore what is holding you back and cultivate freedom and self-love.

## About the Goddess Embodiment Methodology

When we embody the goddess, we allow the [divine feminine](/tag/divine-feminine/) archetype of the goddess to inspire us toward a life of self-empowerment, self-love and confidence. It is radiating love and beauty within and without, while bringing the dark aspects of the goddess into balance as well. For an introduction to the concept, read [What Is an Embodied Goddess?](/what-is-an-embodied-goddess/).

Connecting to the Goddess will help you to shine along your path no matter what you’re struggling with in life. Whether your goals are to get your body back in shape and feel beautiful, navigating a challenging twin flame relationship, finding love, or getting your career on track, goddess embodiment practices will help you to find your direction with confidence and a deep sense of inner peace and knowing.

Embodiment runs throughout Tarot Flower—in Tarot, Goddess work, ritual and [Sacred Becoming](/sacred-becoming/), the larger transformational and alchemical system in which embodiment is fundamental. The free [Goddess Embodiment Guide](/10-steps-to-embody-the-goddess-free-download/) is an entry into practice. For those seeking private guidance, [Sacred Becoming Mentorship](/sacred-becoming/mentorship/) is a separate paid one-to-one container, not a Goddess Embodiment package.
