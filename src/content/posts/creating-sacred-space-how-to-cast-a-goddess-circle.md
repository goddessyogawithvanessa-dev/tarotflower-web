Creating sacred space is a wonderful way to prepare ourselves for ritual, magic, meditation, shadow work and embodiment dance. By casting a Goddess Circle, we transform our physical surroundings into a sanctuary where we can work with the five elements, connect with our intuition and focus our energy on our intentions. Inspired by the third eye chakra and Goddess Theia, we'll explore how to use crystals, flowers, colors and affirmations to create a magical space that nurtures and protects us. And the beauty of learning to create sanctuary is that, eventually, we can carry it with us wherever we go.

## What Is the Third Eye Chakra?

The third eye chakra represents intuition, psychic ability, sacred space, energetic bubbles, the spirit element, the Major Arcana and the High Priestess of the Tarot.

When tuning into our intuition, it is important that we are in a safe space where we will not be bombarded with external stimuli. The voice of intuition often whispers softly, and it can take time to hear her.

Before we begin to listen, we must make sure that external distractions—including other people’s energies—are not interfering. Think of it like a radio channel. It is important that we are tuned in completely, or we may pick up messages that are not our own, or receive them in a garbled way that is difficult to understand.

## Third Eye Chakra Meaning and Intuition

The third eye chakra is a receiver of information that is neither a thought nor a physical experience in the external world. It is a deep inner knowing that simply appears.

People receive intuitive messages in different ways. Some experience visualizations, while others have sudden realizations or hear words in the mind’s ear. However the message comes, the common denominator is that it does not need to be overanalyzed. It is not filled with emotion or thought; it simply arrives. This is one of the hallmark qualities of intuitive knowing.

## What Is a Goddess Circle?

Whether you are preparing a full-moon ritual, a new-moon ritual or a quiet personal practice, a goddess circle is a way of creating sanctuary.

Much like an altar, a goddess circle is a physical representation of an energetic boundary. It can use the five elements to give visible form to the intention and protection of the space.

There is no single correct way to create a goddess circle. The objects matter less than the care and meaning with which you place them. It is helpful, however, to understand which elements offer the kind of energetic support your practice needs.

## How to Cast a Goddess Circle

### 1. Name the intention

Begin by deciding what the circle will hold. You might be creating space for meditation, healing, shadow work, a tarot reading, movement, rest or ritual. A clear intention helps you choose what belongs inside the circle and what can remain outside it.

### 2. Choose and clear the space

Select a place where you can be undisturbed. Tidy it, soften the lighting and remove anything that pulls your attention away. You may open a window, use sound, or work with incense if that is safe and appropriate for your space.

### 3. Mark the boundary

Create the circle with flowers, crystals, candles, fabric, objects from nature or simply your imagination. You can acknowledge the directions or move around the edge of the circle slowly, noticing the moment when ordinary space becomes intentional space.

### 4. Invite the elements

You may represent Earth with soil, stones, flowers or grain; Water with a bowl or cup; Air with incense, feathers or breath; and Fire with a candle. Spirit can be represented by an object that connects you to intuition, divinity or the mystery beyond form.

Only use what feels meaningful and safe. A goddess circle can be abundant or very simple.

### 5. Enter with awareness

Pause before stepping into the circle. Feel the boundary you have created. Let the body understand that you are entering a space for listening, reflection or transformation.

### 6. Close the circle

When the practice is complete, offer gratitude, extinguish candles safely and release the boundary with intention. Put away or return the elements with the same care you used to place them.

## Colors for the Third Eye Chakra

You can incorporate third eye chakra colors such as indigo blue into your goddess circle. Indigo, a deep blue-purple, is associated with spiritual wisdom and psychic ability. Dark blue also corresponds to the third eye chakra and can support wisdom, healing and self-realization.

Specific crystals are traditionally used to support intuitive practice. Lapis lazuli, aquamarine and turquoise are among them. Lapis, for example, has long been regarded as a stone of protection and higher awareness.

Flowers, incense and essential oils can also become part of the circle. Cornflowers have associations with psychic ability, fertility, love, sexuality and abundance. Choose the elements that resonate with you and with the intention you are holding.

## Fortify Sacred Space With Third Eye Chakra Affirmations

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/sacred-space/creating-sacred-space-affirmations-sanctuary-928.webp" srcset="/images/sacred-space/creating-sacred-space-affirmations-sanctuary-480.webp 480w, /images/sacred-space/creating-sacred-space-affirmations-sanctuary-928.webp 928w" sizes="(max-width: 820px) calc(100vw - 40px), 680px" width="928" height="1296" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Serene goddess with purple fruit offerings, creating sacred space, third eye chakra affirmations, spiritual protection, intuition, inner sanctuary, Divine Feminine, Sacred Becoming Mentorship, Midjourney art Vanessa Hylande, Tarot Flower."></figure>

Affirmations can help fortify and protect a sanctuary. You might speak them while walking the circle, write them in your journal or repeat them quietly before beginning your practice.

For example:

- My intuition guides me.
- I create safety within and around me.
- I listen to the quiet wisdom of my inner voice.
- I know what belongs in my sacred space.
- I carry sanctuary within me wherever I go.

We need to create sacred space internally as well as in physical reality. Once we learn to do both, we can take that sanctuary with us. In times of chaos, we can find calm. In times of stress, we can find peace. In times of loneliness, we can find connection.
