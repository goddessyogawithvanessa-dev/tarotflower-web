## You can step into the sacred circle with me.

### Initiation — I Choose

Let’s create our own Goddess Circle as we walk the circle three times three and create our sacred space. Let’s dive deeply into the Sacred Becoming Oracle archetypes of Fire and begin our journey inward.

**What are you ready to become?**

This is a space for radical honesty, authenticity, and irreverence at the altar. A sacred space to turn inward, reflect, journal, and make a choice.

### Destruction — I Release

**What are you ready to release?**

Now we’ll bring that choice into the ritual. Into the fire. With you beside me, we’ll step through the threshold together and transmute shadow into light through our ritual.

### Guardian — I Embody

Now it’s time to dance it out.

**Be free. Be wild. Be the Warrior Goddess.**

You can follow my movement or follow your own. There is no right or wrong way. Let’s move the energy through our bodies and through the space, expanding it, radiating it, magnifying the Fire and the power within us.

Then together, we’ll gather it back in and ground it into the earth.

### Freedom — I Receive

And then, you receive.

You’ve chosen. You’ve released. You’ve moved the energy through your body and grounded it back into the earth.

**Now it’s your turn to simply surrender.**

As you lie back, I’ll keep holding our channel open, receiving the divine messages and transforming them through my voice into sound and song.

**This blessing is for you.**

Let it reverberate through space, time, body and energy.

**All you have to do is receive it.**
